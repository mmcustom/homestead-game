# Homestead
## Catfish v1.0

Status: Design Draft

---

# Purpose

Catfish is the homestead's biggest single catch — the reward for a player who invests in passive infrastructure rather than time at the water's edge. Confirmed 2026-09-22 (Mike).

---

# Availability

- Moderate, per Fishing_System.md.

---

# Activity

- No hard time-of-day gating — Catfish can be caught day or night, unlike Crappie's strict dawn/dusk/night requirement.
- Easier catch at night or in low light, reflecting real feeding behavior, without locking the species behind a window the way Crappie is.

Real-world grounding: catfish are scent/barbel-driven bottom scavengers comfortable feeding in low light or murky water, and are commonly targeted specifically at night because of this. See Research.md, Fish Species Behavior Grounding.

---

# Locations

- Bottom-oriented, deep or murky water. Not restricted to one location type the way Crappie (Deep Holes) or Bass (Fallen Trees) are — Deep Holes and Creek Bends both fit.

---

# Catch Method

- Especially effective with Fish Traps — confirmed 2026-09-22 (Mike). Catfish is a scent-driven scavenger, the mirror image of Bass's sight-hunting behavior, and is the natural fit for Fishing_System.md's passive Fish Trap loop rather than active Rod and Reel fishing.
- This makes Catfish the homestead's "set it and forget it" fish — the counterpart to Bass being the active-fishing prize.

Real-world grounding: catfish use scent and barbels rather than sight to locate food, distinct from Bass's sight-based hunting. See Research.md, Fish Species Behavior Grounding.

---

# Yield

- 4 Food units per catch — confirmed 2026-09-22 (Mike). The top of the four-species yield scale: Bluegill 1, Crappie 2, Bass 3, Catfish 4.

---

# Preservation

- Standard — Cook, Smoke, or Dry, same as any catch. No special case.

---

# Design Rules

1. Catfish's advantage/drawback pair: the biggest single-catch yield of the four species, earned through passive Fish Trap infrastructure rather than active fishing skill — the direct counterpart to Bass. Confirmed 2026-09-22 (Mike).

2. Catfish should stay flexible on time of day (no hard gating) even if a night/low-light catch-rate bonus is added later — it shouldn't turn into a second Crappie-style locked window.

3. Keep Catfish's yield as the ceiling of the four-species scale if any of the other three numbers change.
