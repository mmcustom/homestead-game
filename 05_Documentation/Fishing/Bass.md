# Homestead
## Largemouth Bass v1.0

Status: Design Draft

---

# Purpose

Bass is the homestead's prized catch — a bigger reward than Bluegill or Crappie, but one that specifically rewards active fishing rather than the passive Fish Trap loop. Confirmed 2026-09-22 (Mike).

---

# Availability

- Moderate, per Fishing_System.md.

---

# Activity

- Daylight — Bass is a sight hunter and feeds primarily when it can see its prey, unlike Crappie's low-light pattern or Catfish's night-leaning one.

Real-world grounding: largemouth bass are sight-hunting ambush predators that feed mainly during daylight and respond to visible movement rather than scent. See Research.md, Fish Species Behavior Grounding.

---

# Locations

- Cover-oriented — logs, weed lines, fallen trees. Maps to "Fallen Trees" in Fishing_System.md's existing Fishing Locations list.

---

# Catch Method

- Bass does not work well with the passive Fish Trap method — confirmed 2026-09-22 (Mike). Fish Traps rely on bait/scent, but Bass hunts by sight and keys off active lure movement, so it should be effectively Rod-and-Reel-only (or heavily reduced on traps) rather than something a player can passively farm the way Bluegill or Catfish can.
- This makes Bass the one species that specifically rewards active fishing over passive infrastructure.

Real-world grounding: Bass respond to active presentation rather than passive scent, unlike the scent/barbel-driven feeding of Catfish. See Research.md, Fish Species Behavior Grounding.

---

# Yield

- 3 Food units per catch — confirmed 2026-09-22 (Mike). Deliberately a notch below Catfish's yield, so the two "Moderate availability" species don't feel interchangeable: Bass is the harder-won, actively-fished prize; Catfish is the biggest single catch but earned differently (see Catfish.md).

---

# Preservation

- Standard — Cook, Smoke, or Dry, same as any catch. No special case.

---

# Design Rules

1. Bass's advantage/drawback pair: strong yield and a "desirable catch" identity, but effectively locked out of the passive Fish Trap loop — it has to be actively fished. Confirmed 2026-09-22 (Mike).

2. Bass should stay the one species where Rod and Reel (or Cane Pole, at reduced effectiveness) meaningfully outperforms Fish Traps, so the Fishing Methods section of Fishing_System.md has a real species-level reason to matter, not just a generic tradeoff.

3. Keep Bass's yield below Catfish's if either number changes — the two shouldn't collapse into the same "moderate fish, big yield" identity.
