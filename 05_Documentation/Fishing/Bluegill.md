# Homestead
## Bluegill v1.0

Status: Design Draft

---

# Purpose

Bluegill is the most reliable fish on the homestead — the species a player leans on when they need food and don't want to think hard about it. Confirmed 2026-09-22 (Mike).

---

# Availability

- Common — the most abundant of the four Alpha 0.1 fish species, per Fishing_System.md.

---

# Activity

- Active throughout the day. No dawn/dusk or nighttime gating — unlike Crappie and Catfish, Bluegill feed aggressively at any hour, which is what makes them the dependable choice.

Real-world grounding: bluegill are aggressive, opportunistic feeders that stay active all day, distinct from the dawn/dusk/night pattern of Crappie and Catfish. See Research.md, Fish Species Behavior Grounding.

---

# Locations

- Shallow water near shoreline vegetation — Pond Banks, per Fishing_System.md's existing Fishing Locations list.
- No structure-seeking or deep-water behavior the way Crappie and Bass show.

---

# Yield

- 1 Food unit (small) per catch — confirmed 2026-09-22 (Mike). The baseline "small" tier among the four species; Catfish and Bass yield more per catch, in exchange for being harder to find or catch consistently.

---

# Catch Difficulty

- Easiest catch of the four species. Works reliably with Cane Pole (Fishing_System.md's cheapest, least effective method) as well as Rod and Reel — Bluegill is the entry point that doesn't punish a player for using primitive gear.

---

# Preservation

- Standard — Cook, Smoke, or Dry, same as any catch. No special case.

---

# Design Rules

1. Bluegill's advantage is reliability, not yield: easiest catch, works with the cheapest gear, active all day. Its drawback is the smallest yield per catch of the four species. Confirmed 2026-09-22 (Mike).

2. Bluegill should never be gated behind time of day, location discovery, or season the way Crappie, Bass, and Catfish are — it's the fallback fish.

3. If Bluegill's numbers ever change, keep the gap between it and the other three species intentional — it should stay the fastest, most forgiving catch even if the yield number moves.
