# Homestead
## Crappie v1.0

Status: Design Draft

---

# Purpose

Crappie rewards a player who's learned to read water and track the calendar — a bigger, better catch than Bluegill for anyone willing to fish deeper water on the right schedule, with one dramatic seasonal exception. Confirmed 2026-09-22 (Mike).

---

# Availability

- Seasonal, per Fishing_System.md — see Spring Spawn Window below for what that means mechanically.

---

# Activity

- Dawn, dusk, and night — the opposite pattern from Bluegill. Crappie hunt small baitfish and are most active in low light.

Real-world grounding: crappie hunt baitfish and are most active at dawn, dusk, and night, distinct from Bluegill's all-day activity. See Research.md, Fish Species Behavior Grounding.

---

# Locations

- Deeper water near submerged structure — brush piles, drop-offs. Maps to "Deep Holes" in Fishing_System.md's existing Fishing Locations list.
- Outside the Spring Spawn Window (below), Crappie stay deep and structure-oriented rather than near shore.

---

# Spring Spawn Window

Confirmed 2026-09-22 (Mike) — modeled as a real behavior shift, not just a flavor label on the "Seasonal" tag:

- During Spring, Crappie school up and move into shallow water near shoreline cover to spawn — the same kind of shallow water Bluegill occupies year-round.
- While in the Spring Spawn Window, Crappie are dramatically easier to locate and catch than at any other time of year — higher catch rate, and a player who finds one is likely finding several (schooling behavior).
- Outside Spring, Crappie return to their standard Deep Holes / structure-oriented, dawn-dusk-night pattern above, and are correspondingly harder to find and catch.

Real-world grounding: crappie are documented schooling spawners that move to shallow water in spring specifically to spawn, becoming far easier to catch than during the rest of the year. See Research.md, Fish Species Behavior Grounding.

---

# Yield

- 2 Food units per catch — larger than Bluegill's 1, smaller than Bass and Catfish. Confirmed 2026-09-22 (Mike).

---

# Preservation

- Standard — Cook, Smoke, or Dry, same as any catch. No special case.

---

# Design Rules

1. Crappie's advantage/drawback pair: normally harder to find (deep, structure-bound, low-light only) and better-yielding than Bluegill, but becomes the easiest, most abundant catch on the homestead for one season — Spring. That swing is the point; don't smooth it out for the sake of consistency.

2. The Spring Spawn Window is a real behavior shift, not cosmetic — it should actually change where and how easily Crappie are caught in Spring, per Mike's explicit confirmation, not just a label.

3. Outside Spring, Crappie's Deep Holes / dawn-dusk-night pattern should feel meaningfully different from Bluegill's all-day shallow-water pattern — the two fish shouldn't compete for the same time and place except during the Spring window.
