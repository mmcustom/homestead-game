# Homestead
## Plants: Fruit v1.0

Status: Design Draft

---

# Purpose

Concrete, tunable stats for Wild Apples and Pawpaw, the two Fruit species defined in Foraging_System.md. This document does not redefine the general Foraging mechanic — it's the per-species numbers that mechanic needs to actually run.

---

# Design Role

Fruit is a Fall resource per Foraging_System.md, arriving alongside Nuts as part of the "most important storage season." Unlike Berries (Summer, fresh/light), Fruit leans toward preservation for the Winter ahead — though, per Mike's confirmation below, not evenly across both species.

---

# Wild Apples

- **Season:** Fall (per Foraging_System.md)
- **Yield per harvest:** 4 units per tree — confirmed 2026-09-22 (Mike)
- **Regrowth:** Annual, same tree available again next Fall (consistent with Berries.md's annual-patch precedent)
- **Uses:** Food, Preservation (dried) — per Foraging_System.md
- **Shelf life / Preservation outcome:** Good — dries well, holds up as a genuine Winter reserve item alongside Nuts
- **Nutritional role:** Moderate — more substantial than a Berry, still not a primary meal on its own

---

# Pawpaw

- **Season:** Fall, same window as Wild Apples — also called out in Research.md as a genuine "last harvest window of the year" item
- **Yield per harvest:** 3 units per tree — confirmed 2026-09-22 (Mike)
- **Regrowth:** Annual
- **Uses:** Food, Preservation
- **Shelf life / Preservation outcome:** Shorter than Wild Apples, confirmed 2026-09-22 (Mike) — real pawpaw is soft-fleshed and doesn't dry or store the way apples do, so it should spoil faster and give a worse Preservation outcome. This makes Pawpaw the "eat it soon, don't count on it for Winter" half of the Fruit category, while Wild Apples is the "stock it up" half.
- **Nutritional role:** Similar tier to Wild Apples while fresh

---

# Design Rules

1. Values on this page are confirmed baselines, not immutable — normal playtesting-driven tuning still applies.

2. Fruit and Nuts.md together form the Fall "storage season" resources per Foraging_System.md, but within Fruit itself, only Wild Apples earns the "stores well" reputation — Pawpaw is deliberately the fast-spoiling exception. Any future Fruit addition should pick one lane or the other rather than defaulting to "stores well."

3. Annual regrowth follows Berries.md's precedent.
