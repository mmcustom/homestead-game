# Homestead
## Fishing System v1.0

Status: Design Draft

---

# Purpose

Fishing is one of the primary Year One food gathering activities.

Fishing provides:

- Reliable food
- Knowledge progression
- Seasonal gameplay
- Food preservation opportunities

Fishing should reward observation and knowledge rather than random chance.

---

# Core Philosophy

A knowledgeable fisherman catches more fish.

Players should learn:

- Productive locations
- Seasonal behavior
- Water conditions
- Fish species preferences

Knowledge should outperform luck.

---

# Fishing Methods

## Rod and Reel

Primary active fishing method.

Requirements:

- Rod
- Line
- Hook
- Bait

Advantages:

- Player control
- Flexible

Disadvantages:

- Requires time

---

## Cane Pole

Early primitive fishing option.

Advantages:

- Cheap
- Accessible

Disadvantages:

- Reduced effectiveness

---

## Fish Traps

Primary passive fishing method.

Advantages:

- Operate while player performs other tasks

Disadvantages:

- Require placement and maintenance
- Ineffective on Bass — Bass is a sight hunter that responds to active lure presentation, not passive bait/scent. See 05_Documentation/Fishing/Bass.md.
- Especially effective on Catfish — a scent-driven bottom scavenger, the natural fit for passive gear. See 05_Documentation/Fishing/Catfish.md.

---

# Fish Trap Loop

Build Trap
↓
Place Trap
↓
Wait
↓
Check Trap
↓
Harvest Catch

Fish traps support homestead efficiency.

They should supplement active fishing, not replace it.

# Fish Species

## Bluegill

Availability:

- Common

Benefits:

- Easy to catch
- Reliable food (smallest yield of the four species, active all day — see 05_Documentation/Fishing/Bluegill.md)

---

## Catfish

Availability:

- Moderate

Benefits:

- Large food yield (biggest single catch of the four species; works especially well in Fish Traps — see 05_Documentation/Fishing/Catfish.md)

---

## Bass

Availability:

- Moderate

Benefits:

- Desirable catch (effectively Rod and Reel / Cane Pole only — see 05_Documentation/Fishing/Bass.md)

---

## Crappie

Availability:

- Seasonal (schools move to shallow water to spawn in Spring, becoming dramatically easier to catch — see 05_Documentation/Fishing/Crappie.md for the Spring Spawn Window)

Benefits:

- Good food source

# Season Effects

## Spring

Fish activity increases.

Excellent fishing opportunities.

---

## Summer

Strong fishing.

Early morning and evening become important.

---

## Fall

Fish actively feed.

High harvest potential.

---

## Winter

Fishing becomes difficult.

Open water locations remain valuable.

Future ice fishing may be possible.

---

# Weather Effects

Rain:

- Increased fish activity in some locations

Heat:

- Reduced daytime activity

Cold Fronts:

- Variable behavior

Weather should influence fishing decisions.

---

# Food Preservation

Caught fish can be:

- Cooked
- Smoked
- Dried

Preservation becomes increasingly important during fall.

---

# Economy Integration

Fish are primarily food.

Excess fish may be:

- Sold
- Traded
- Preserved

Fishing supports survival before income.

---

# Design Rules

1. Fishing should be dependable.

2. Knowledge should improve catch rates.

3. Fish traps supplement active fishing.

4. Seasons should matter.

5. Fishing supports survival first and economy second.
