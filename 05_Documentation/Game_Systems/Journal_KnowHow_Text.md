# Journal Know-how Entries: Text (draft 2026-10-27)

Written by Claude (docs) for Claude Code to use verbatim in Economy/KnowHowRules.cs. Plain player-facing wording. Keys written as the game shows them ("hold E" where the game asks for a hold). If a number or key in the code differs from the text, tell Mike instead of changing the meaning.

The Journal screen gets a seventh section, Know-how. Each entry unlocks the first time its trigger happens (see the plan: first item held, first vessel hung, first site placed).

---

## 1. Ash: Collect and Sell

Trigger: first Ash in the pack.

Burning wood in a campfire or hearth leaves Ash behind. At the fire, press G to collect it (this works whether the fire is lit or out). Firewood leaves 1 Ash for every 3 burned, and Sticks, Branches and Logs leave some too.

Ash sells at the Trading Post for about $2 each, and the price drops a little with every one you sell, then recovers over the next days. You can also leach Ash into Lye, which is worth much more as Potash (see "From Ash to Potash").

## 2. Kindling Bundles

Trigger: first Kindling Bundle held.

Felling trees leaves you plenty of Sticks. In the Craft tab, 10 Sticks make 1 Kindling Bundle, which sells for about $1. Use the amount control to craft several at once.

Sticks also burn and leave a little Ash (1 Ash for every 12 Sticks).

## 3. The Tripod and Boiling Water

Trigger: first Tripod held, or a Cooking Pot first hung.

Boiling needs the Cooking Pot hung on a tripod over a lit fire. Carrying the Pot is not enough.

1. Craft a Tripod (Craft tab, Fire and Light: 3 Branches and 1 Cordage).
2. Open Build, then Other, and click Set Up. Aim at a campfire or hearth within 3 m and click when the ghost is green. The Grill cannot take a tripod.
3. Aim at the tripod and hold E to hang the Cooking Pot from your pack.
4. Stand within 3 m of the lit fire, open Inventory (I) and press Boil on the water.

One vessel hangs at a time, and holding E swaps or takes it down. To dismantle the tripod, put the fire out, equip the Hammer and hold R; the Tripod and the Pot come back to you.

## 4. Tapping Maples

Trigger: first Spile or Sap Bucket held.

Maples run sap only in Winter and Spring, on days that thaw after a freezing night.

1. Craft a Spile (3 Sticks) and a Sap Bucket (1 Log, 3 Branches and 1 Cordage) for each tap.
2. Equip the Spile. While you hold it, a green droplet floats over each Maple within 80 m that you can tap.
3. Walk up and click the Maple. One tap per tree, up to 12.

Your taps show a pail marker, visible from far away and on the minimap: grey while filling, amber when 4 L or more is ready, brown-red when the sap has soured. Hold E at a tap to collect, and R to take the tap down. Sap sours after 48 hours in the bucket, so check your taps about once a day.

## 5. Sap to Syrup

Trigger: first Maple Sap held.

10 L of sap boils down to 1 Maple Syrup in about 12 seconds. You need the Cooking Pot hung on a tripod over a lit fire, and you must stand within 3 m of it. Open Inventory (I), find the Maple Sap row and press Boil. It uses no extra Firewood.

Carried sap does not spoil. Syrup sells for about $10 at the Trading Post, and you can eat it too.

## 6. Using the Leach Hopper

Trigger: Leach Hopper site first placed.

A Leach Hopper turns Ash into Lye. Build it from the Build tab: place the site, deliver 3 Logs and 4 Tall Grass by hand with R (the site holds them between trips), then equip the Hammer and hold click for about 20 blows.

To load it, put the Hammer away and press R at the hopper. Each Lye takes 1 Ash and 1 L of water (the poorest water goes in first), up to 12 at a time. It leaches for 24 game hours, and sleeping counts. Hold E at the hopper to collect the Lye. With the Hammer equipped, R dismantles the hopper instead and gives back half the materials, with any Ash or Lye inside.

## 7. From Ash to Potash

Trigger: first Ash, Lye or Potash Kettle held, or a Leach Hopper site first placed.

Potash sells for far more than the Ash it comes from: about $5 of Potash per Ash, against $2 for the raw Ash. The chain has three steps.

1. Leach. Build a Leach Hopper (see "Using the Leach Hopper"), load Ash and water, and wait 24 game hours.
2. Collect. Hold E at the hopper to take the Lye. Lye also sells by itself for about $3, but it is worth more as Potash.
3. Boil. Buy a Potash Kettle at the Trading Post ($25). Set up a tripod over a lit fire and hold E at it to hang the Kettle. Open Inventory (I) and press Boil on the Lye row: 3 Lye and 2 Firewood make 1 Potash in 18 seconds. The Firewood is used up and leaves no Ash.

Sell the Potash at the Trading Post (about $16 each).
