## 05_Documentation/Livestock

Status: Done (3/3 Alpha 0.1 species confirmed)

Purpose (confirmed 2026-09-22): per-species data sheets (concrete stats — feed consumption, milk yield, breeding cooldown, and similar) for each livestock animal. Distinct from Game_Systems/Livestock_System.md, which covers the general mechanic and is done.

Docs added and confirmed 2026-09-22 by Claude, via a propose-then-confirm pattern (Claude drafts real-world-grounded numbers, Mike confirms or adjusts before they're locked):

- Goats.md — Slow breeder (Spring-only, 1 kid/cycle, 30-day maturation), high-value ongoing milk. Missed milking reduces yield rather than spoiling it.
- Chickens.md — Mid-speed maturation (20 days), unconditional daily eggs (not breeding-gated, unlike goat milk), halved egg output in Winter, non-seasonal hatching. Culling a hen for meat has no downside beyond the lost future eggs.
- Rabbits.md — Fastest breeder of the three by explicit design direction (15-day maturation, ~20-day full breeding cycle, 4 kits/litter), no ongoing daily resource between litters. Shares Trapping_System.md's "Small Furs" resource rather than a separate one. Population is intentionally uncapped — regulated by Livestock_System.md's existing feed-shortage pressure, not a hard limit.

Design principle established 2026-09-22 (Mike): each livestock type has a clear, non-overlapping advantage/drawback rather than similar stats with different names.

Owner: Claude

2026-09-24 — Claude Code — Investigated the "rain ambience during clear, sunny weather" bug. The audio was correct and the visuals were wrong. It was the bug entry's cause (2), but it lives in the rendering rather than WeatherManager. Mike's save at the time of the report had WeatherManager in Heavy Rain (`rainedToday: true`, mid-morning), and AudioManager was correctly playing the heavy-rain loop. Switching weather to Clear in Play Mode faded rain out and Day ambience in, as Audio_System.md specifies. The sky looked sunny because weather had no visible effect. DayNightCycle dimmed the sun (to 20% in heavy rain), but the scene's automatic histogram exposure brightened the image straight back up, and there were no clouds or weather fog. Fix in `DayNightCycle.cs`: a runtime-only weather volume (created in Play Mode, so no profile asset is modified) sets three things per weather type, blending over in-game time like the sun dimming already did. (a) Fog distance, per Weather_System.md's visibility effects: Clear 400m, Cloudy 300m, Light Rain 200m, Heavy Rain 120m, Thunderstorm 80m, Cold Front 350m, Snow 150m, Wind 400m. (b) Cloud cover, from none when clear to fully overcast in rain, storms and snow, using HDRP's Cloud Layer. (c) Exposure compensation, so 70% of the sun's weather dimming actually shows on screen. Also moved the fog's base height from 0m (tuned for the old flat ground) to 20m, because the new terrain sits 15–35m up where the old fog had mostly faded. All values are Inspector-tunable on Day Night Cycle. Verified with before/after captures from the same spot: heavy rain is now a dark, overcast, hazy day, and clear weather looks as it did before. Mike's slot1 was backed up and restored byte-for-byte after every Play Mode run. Stopping Play Mode auto-saves over the slot, and one run's auto-save did overwrite it before the restore. Not addressed: there are still no rain or snow particles. Weather_System.md doesn't call for them, so they'd be a new feature for Claude/Mike to decide on. Not committed yet.
