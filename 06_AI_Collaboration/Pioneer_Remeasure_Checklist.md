# Pioneer Re-measure Run (Money Phase D2)

**Purpose:** the last full-year Pioneer run (2026-10-28 to 10-29) earned about $5.58 a game day against the $20.48 a day the payment needs, and missed the first payment by $121.69. Since then the Workshop (Rough Stool, End Table, Chair, Table), pottery and metalwork were built. This run measures what a Pioneer earns NOW, so the tier numbers (missed payments before a loss, late fee, interest rate, sell share) can be set from facts.

**Not a pass/fail test.** It is a data run. Nothing here can "fail"; I just need the exports and a few notes.

Plan and rules: `Income_Measuring_Plan.md`. Results go into `Measurements\Income_Measuring_Log.md`.

## Rules (same as before)

- New game: **Pioneer, 5 acres, $0 savings**. Any weapon.
- Play like a real player. **No F12, no cash keys, no F11 starting kit.** (F8 season skip is not allowed either: the days must be played.)
- Speed keys are fine. **Use 10x, not 60x** (60x made Winter unreliable last time). **Drop to 1x or 10x from about day 100** so you do not roll past day 112 without the export.
- Build what a new player needs first (shelter, water, fire, food), then earn however you like. Use the Workshop, pottery, Smithy, Maple, Potash and snares as you would in a real game. You do not have to use all of them.
- Do not sell the starting gear back to the Trading Post just to pad income (it counted as income last time and is not repeatable).

## Run steps

1. Start a new game, Pioneer, 5 acres. Note the real clock time you start.
2. Play through year 1. **Press End at each of these points** (the HUD shows where the CSV is written):
   - end of Spring (day 28)
   - end of Summer (day 56)
   - end of Autumn (day 84)
   - **day 110 or 111**, before the first payment on day 112 (this is the one we missed last time)
3. After day 112, press End once more, and press L to read the Winter statement.
4. Keep going into Spring Year 2 only if you want to; it is optional. If you do, press End at the end of Spring Y2.

I work out income per game day by season, the coverage against the $20.48 payment, and what sold best. Then I propose the D2 tier numbers (including whether the Pioneer loss threshold is 2 or 3), and you decide. Nothing in the game changes until you approve.
