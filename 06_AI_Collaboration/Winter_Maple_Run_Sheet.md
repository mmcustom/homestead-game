# Winter Maple Measuring Run: Run Sheet

Purpose: measure what Maple Syrup adds in one Winter, which the Winter Pioneer run did not test (it earned $63.99 in Winter with no Syrup at all).

Build: a3f5f07 (code). Date written: 2026-10-29.

## Rules

- Start a NEW game on the Pioneer tier, 5 acres.
- Press F11 once at the start for the starting kit (gives the Tripod, Hammer and Pot). Then press F8 once to jump to Winter.
- Dev keys allowed: F11 once, F8 once, F7 speed (10x at most, never 60x), End to export. Nothing else.
- NOT allowed: F5 skip day, F6, F12, PageUp (free money), Home, Shift+Home, Delete, Shift+Delete, Page Down, Ctrl+Delete, Ctrl+Insert, Insert.
- Play normally for the 28 days of Winter: hunt, trap, gather, sell as you like. The point is to see what Maple adds on top of a normal Winter, so try to tap Maples as soon as you can.
- Do not stop and start the game part way. If you must quit, tell me what day it was.
- The game's first loan payment arrives at the end of Winter (day 112). Take the End export BEFORE the season rolls over (about Winter day 26 to 27).

## Setup steps

1. Craft Spiles (3 Sticks each) and Sap Buckets (1 Log + 3 Branches + 1 Cordage each). Equip the Spile and tap Maples (one tap per tree, 12 max).
2. Hang the Pot on the Tripod over a lit campfire (Build tab, Other, Set Up). Boil sap there: 10 L of sap makes 1 Syrup.
3. Sell Syrup at the market (about $10 each, price drops 5% per unit sold).

## End of run

1. On about Winter day 26 to 27, press End to export the ledger CSVs. I will copy them to the Measurements folder as `maple_winter_ledger.csv` and `maple_winter_ledger_items.csv`.
2. Open L and screenshot the Winter statement (both screenshots, plain and maximized Game tab).
3. Answer five questions:
   1. How many real minutes of work was the Maple chain each game day, roughly?
   2. Was the Pot and Tripod setup clear without the checklist?
   3. Is boiling worth it against what else you could do with that time?
   4. What was the biggest blocker?
   5. Did you notice any bug, clipping or confusing text?

## What I will compute

Syrup per game day, Maple income per game day (the Farm produce line), total Winter income per game day against the $20.48 per game day payment, and how much of the Winter is spent on Maple chores.
